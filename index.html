A saved link is a bookmark with a sticker. Thensday reads what's actually in the video: every frame's on-screen text, what's said out loud, and the caption. Then it tells you what's in it, in one line.

FAQ

Good questions.

Which apps can I share from?

Anything with a Share button: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Safari, Photos, and so on. Reels, posts, links, screenshots, saved videos and plain text all work.

How does it read a reel from just a link?

Your iPhone opens the public page the way a browser would. If the video is publicly served, Thensday reads it on the device — on-screen text, speech, caption — and then deletes it. If a page is private or behind a login, it keeps the caption and lets you add the video from your saved videos or a screen recording.

Is anything uploaded?

No. There's no account and no Thensday server. Reading and AI happen on your iPhone. The App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”. Read the privacy policy.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhones on iOS 18 or later. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Thensday uses Apple's on-device models. Every other iPhone uses a careful built-in rules engine, and every feature still works.

Will it nag me?

No. You get one notification per moment you pick, and none for Someday. There are no streaks, no scores and no counts of unfinished things anywhere in the app.

What happens to things I never get to?

After 30 days untouched, a save quietly moves to Faded. It's never deleted, and you can bring it back with a tap. Letting go is a good outcome, too.

What does Pro cost?

$6.99 a month, or $39.99 a year with a 7-day free trial. Saving, the full read, every action, the four moments and the widget are free with no limit.
