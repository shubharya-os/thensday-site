One-pan lemon chickpeas

Serves 2 · 25 minutes · Weeknight dinner

Crispy chickpeas, a lot of garlic and a squeeze of lemon. One pan, one wooden spoon, dinner done.

Ingredients

  • 400 g chickpeas, drained
  • 1 lemon
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • 1 handful parsley

Method

  • Pat the chickpeas dry. Fry them in the olive oil over medium-high heat for 10 minutes, until golden.
  • Add the sliced garlic and chili flakes and cook for 2 minutes more.
  • Take off the heat. Grate in the lemon zest, squeeze in the juice, and toss with the parsley.
  • Season with salt and eat straight away, with bread or over rice.
