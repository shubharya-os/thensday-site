Thirty seconds. One recipe.

Share a sample page into Thensday and watch it pull out what matters. You'll need Thensday on your iPhone. It's in beta on TestFlight; ask to join.

You're on a computer. Open thensday.com/try in Safari on your iPhone, or send yourself the sample link: thensday.com/sample.html
You're on iPhone. Perfect. Follow the steps below; it takes half a minute.
  1. 1

    Open the sample in Safari

    A real web page for a one-pan lemon chickpea dinner, made for this demo.

    Open the sample
  2. 2

    Tap Share, then Thensday

    Don't see Thensday in the row of apps? Scroll to the end, tap More, switch Thensday on and drag it to the front. Next time it's one tap.

    →
  3. 3

    Tap Save

    Pick a why and a when if you like, say Cook and Next shop, or skip both. Nothing is required.

    CookNext shopSomeday
  4. 4

    Open Thensday

    Your card is there with the dish and its ingredients pulled out, ready to merge into one grocery list for your next shop.

Now try the real thing

Next time a reel, post or video catches your eye in any app, tap its Share button and pick Thensday. It reads the whole thing on your iPhone: what's on screen, what's said and the caption.
